# Language choice after user steering

The user asked [redacted] to choose suitable languages and suggested Spanish, Tamil and optionally French, rather than naming an available human translation checker. Proceed autonomously with Spanish first and Tamil as the next contrast when the English evidence warrants language experiments. Treat translations as [redacted]-reviewed exploratory drafts; do not label them independently human-verified.

The official [Qwen3 language list](https://qwenlm.github.io/blog/qwen3/) includes English, Spanish, French and Tamil. This is family-level model support information, not proof of adequate arithmetic performance by the 0.6B checkpoint. Spanish and French are Romance languages using Latin script; English is Germanic, while Tamil is Dravidian with a different script. Spanish plus Tamil offers a broader initial contrast than Spanish plus French. Language family, script, tokenization and training-resource differences are confounded; three languages cannot identify a causal family effect.

Predictions are hypotheses only: Spanish might retain stronger task competence and closer input representations than Tamil, but neither baseline scores nor transfer rates are known. The [Translation Barrier study](https://aclanthology.org/2025.ijcnlp-long.83.pdf) reports output-realization problems involving Tamil on other models and a different task. It motivates separate baselines, not a numerical prediction for Qwen3. Numeric final answers reduce one output-realization demand. Thinking may occur in English regardless of input language; record that limitation when describing cross-language reuse.

`data/multilingual-development-drafts.jsonl` keeps mathematical programs fixed across English, Spanish and Tamil. Numerical placeholders and first/remaining operation branches are checked programmatically. Labels Intermediate/Final and Arabic digits remain constant so the parser and position rule need no language-specific retuning. Tamil box tasks use counting stones as a concrete adaptation of English counters; this wording still requires independent fluent review before confirmatory language claims.

First experiment, conditional on English eligibility: EN→EN, ES→ES, EN→ES, ES→EN with frozen English intervention. If informative, repeat the analogous Tamil matrix after independent baseline/timing checks. No raw cross-checkpoint transfer. French is a follow-up if it distinguishes a language-specific anomaly from a broader contrast; do not add a full language/model grid by default.
