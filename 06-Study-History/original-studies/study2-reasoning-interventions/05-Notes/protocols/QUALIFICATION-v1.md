# Qualification protocol v1

Declared 21 September 2026 after two native throughput smoke responses, before any AI-04 activation outcomes. Local freeze, not public preregistration.

Use official Qwen3-0.6B revision c1899de289a04d12100db370d81485cdf75e47ca, verified local weight hashes; native thinking template, float32, eager attention, singleton, TF32 off, deterministic PyTorch, temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20. A dedicated environment is pinned by uv.lock. Maximum 768 total assistant tokens, including existing prefix; natural EOS remains active. No answer-now forcing. The two smoke responses measured throughput only and are outside every scientific split.

Before mechanism interpretation: require passive-hook and identity logits max absolute error <=0.0005, exact write locality and identity token equality; custom/native full generated sequences identical on four designated audit prompts. Compare fixed historical patch cached versus full recomputation for the first 24 continuation steps at both first-occurrence and labelled-intermediate boundaries and at layers 13, 20, 26. Full-vocabulary error <=0.0005 and sampled tokens identical. Source replacement must have positive node delta and cause downstream logits to differ by >0.001 in at least one designated case. No-op replay and identity continuation must exactly reproduce their matched stream. Replayed candidate scoring must agree with a separate full-forward teacher-forcing calculation within 0.0005 per token. Native/cached/full-forward shape effects are reported; no gate is changed after desired scientific outcomes.

Four distinct audit programs, separate from pilot/development/final. The audit checks no-op RNG alignment at prefix cuts, including text-inserted prefixes. Parser tests cover thinking exclusion, anchored Final integers, boxed integers, conflicting finals, truncation and invalid output. Parsed final answers require natural completion for success; parsed but truncated answers remain separately visible.

Timing: early is before the first standalone literal true intermediate in generated assistant thinking, if token-aligned. Late is before the Intermediate numeral after </think>. This is an observable timing definition, not a claim about when a latent calculation occurred. Save prior mentions of intermediate and final answer and exact character/token spans. A missing boundary is unavailable, never silently dropped.

The intended pilot is reduced from the launch illustration to 8 new groups x 2 boundaries x 4 conditions x 2 continuation streams (128 attempts), based on the observed approximately 50 tokens/sec serial throughput and approximately 500-token baseline length. Conditions are unchanged, donor block-26 replacement, complete-value text insertion, and one magnitude-matched isotropic direction per group/boundary. Identity is an audit. The later development search may compare layers 13,20,26. Main sample size, selectors and expanded controls are not yet fixed.

Three assays: A is the donor-leading-digit minus recipient-leading-digit immediate logit margin and its change. B sums log probabilities of each entire candidate integer plus native EOS at the clean trace's Final numeral position, while every intervening token from the cut to that position is held at the original recipient value. Save raw candidate scores and patched-minus-clean counterfactual/recipient margin. C regenerates all tokens after the historical patch and counts complete semantic answers; the RNG stream is coupled at the same assistant token index. B explicitly clamps future intermediates; it is neither a natural rollout nor a direct probability estimate for C. No logit-to-probability calibration is assumed.

Clean donor, clean recipient and explicitly supplied-intermediate baselines must each achieve >=80% natural-completion semantic accuracy over the full intended pilot denominator before site localization. Retain every failure and report the jointly competent subset separately. The strict format and permissive semantic parser are reported separately. A supplied-intermediate prompt removes the first-stage computation and directly asks for the recipient remaining operation; its success establishes a behavioral capability, not that an activation carries it.
