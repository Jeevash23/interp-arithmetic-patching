# Development and final planning v1

21 September 2026, after the complete eight-group pilot and before development outcomes. Pilot block 26 copied donor first digits in 32/32 branches, complete donor numerals in 0/32, and complete counterfactual answers in 0/32. Early traces include changed intermediates followed by recomputation; 12/16 have the operational changed-numeral/later-original/recipient-final signature. This extends the late readout question to actual first printed occurrences, while not establishing new priority or a unique mechanism. Pilot v1's failed norm check and the separately corrected random-operator qualification are retained. Original control tolerance is unchanged.

Development uses all 24 generated development groups (not pilot or final), one baseline source stream and two recipient continuation streams. Six candidates cross first_z/label_z with layers 13,20,26. Measure A/B/C for unchanged and activation conditions on every candidate, with all unavailable/truncated/invalid rows retained. Clean donor, recipient and supplied competence must each be >=80%. The first_z label means first literal intermediate mention, not first internal computation; save context and prior final-answer mentions.

Replay selector: eligible sites require B scores on >=80% of intended groups. Rank mean patched-minus-clean counterfactual-minus-recipient complete-candidate log probability margin over available groups. Scores within 0.01 nats of the largest mean are tied; break ties by the fixed candidate order first_z L13,L20,L26, then label_z L13,L20,L26. The 0.01 deadband avoids selecting tiny numerical differences; it is not a calibration to generation probability.

Continuation selector: rank all-denominator counterfactual rate, then fewer noncompletions/invalid/unavailable, then the same fixed order. Both selectors access known donor/recipient task semantics in development. Neither may choose a site separately for a final example. A shared selected site is reported as identical policies; do not replace the primary comparison to manufacture disagreement.

Proceed to a main comparison after qualification even if activation success remains zero or replay and continuation agree. The new question is a held-out comparison of these explicitly defined assays and first-mention versus labelled-output behavior. Donor/first-digit controls will test whether any apparent transfer is separable from copying. No other layers, intervention strengths or outcome-driven task repair will be searched in this version.

Final allocation, fixed before any final model outcomes: first 64 of the generated 80 final groups, balanced over templates 2/3; remaining 16 stay unexamined. New numeric ranges and wordings change together, so this tests that combined shift, not isolated template or width causation. The two frozen selectors each receive two continuation streams. Evaluate unchanged and activation, retaining duplicates explicitly if selectors coincide. Primary endpoint is the paired per-program mean counterfactual rate difference, continuation-selected minus replay-selected. Group bootstrap (10,000 draws, seed 942001), stratified over the two fixed templates; sites and branches stay paired. This is one primary contrast; all other intervals are descriptive, without broad significance claims. The generator's program groups are independent random constructions conditional on fixed template and operator structure; model and templates are not independent population samples.

Precision rationale: with zero successful groups among 64, the one-sided 95% binomial upper bound for a group having any success under the two-stream policy is 4.57%; the mean branch-success rate cannot exceed that group probability. This can bound a 10-percentage-point success claim in a null setting. The pilot's zero effect is not treated as a zero-variance power estimate. Paired bootstrap precision may be poorer if final heterogeneity is higher; report it. No sample extension based on final effect direction.

Controls: reserve first 16 final groups for fixed layer-26, both-boundary first-digit/full-value text and three independent magnitude-matched random directions, two streams each. Keep these separate from selector evaluation. Donor-semantic controls on first eight development groups: same intermediate/different donor answer, same value/different first-step decomposition, different value/same donor answer, and same-leading-digit donor of different width. Controls are explanatory, not extra independent samples or a new test selection bank. Known donor semantics, expression/value observational collisions and source competence remain explicit.

Replication: attempt eight-group two-lookup state composition after its own competence check and a bounded second checkpoint qualification. These are exploratory scope checks. Do not pool task/model results with the primary estimate. A failed competence or numerical gate yields a qualified boundary, not a mechanism claim.

Stop once the declared main comparison, control checks and feasible replication gates are complete. Preserve implementation failures and explicitly distinguish natural completion, budget exhaustion, semantic answer and numerical consistency. No paid compute, public release or submission.
