# Donor semantic panel: completed

The predeclared eight development programs generated 32 alternative source baselines and 64 intervention branches. All 64 branches completed naturally and began with the new source's leading digit. Thirty of 32 source baselines had correct final answers; all were retained. The source errors were development-004/different_z_same_answer (73-7 reported as 63) and development-006/same_z_decomposition (76+8 reported as 92).

Changing the answer at fixed z, changing the decomposition at fixed z, or widening z while retaining its leading digit each preserved all 8/8 original activation sequences at each boundary. No widened-value branch reached its own target; one at each boundary instead kept the original narrower-value target. Different-z/same-answer sources matched 3/8 early and 4/8 late original sequences and reached zero own targets. That variant changes the leading digit in four of eight groups, so it does not isolate full-value effects from digit identity.

These are repeated diagnostic measurements on eight development programs, not a new held-out sample. New supplied-intermediate baselines were not collected for every changed donor; variant-specific target failure alone therefore cannot distinguish transfer from downstream competence. Sequence invariance under the wider sources is the more direct observation. Full source programs, prefixes, residual hashes and exact token sequences are saved in the run journal. Final authoritative diagnostics are in secondary-v1/secondary.json; the earlier donor-secondary-v1.json is a completed-panel extraction before the added source-stage annotation.
